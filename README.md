Everyday Things, Two Lines, Longer Run: explain one everyday thing to a 10-year-old in two lines. `main.py` (Python 3.13, stdlib only, deterministic) writes exactly two lines to `/output/entry.txt`.

The entry:

> A toaster is a tiny sunbed where bread lies down for a tan and pops up, brown and toasty, when its time is up.
> The sunbed maps heat slowly browning the bread, but it leaves out that bread never picks its tan — a spring, not the bread, decides when to pop up.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
